## What Counts as Evidence

A response counts only when it identifies:

1. A real operational workflow.
2. A recurring problem.
3. A measurable cost, delay or risk.
4. The current workaround or tool.
5. Willingness to review, test, pilot or purchase a solution.

Encouragement, introductions and general advice are not customer validation.
